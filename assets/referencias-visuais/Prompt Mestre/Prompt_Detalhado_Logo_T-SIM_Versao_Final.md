# Prompt Mestre Detalhado — Logo T-SIM (Versão Final Refinada)

Este arquivo contém o **prompt detalhado** da versão final criada para a marca **T-SIM**, com estética premium, tecnológica, futurista e acabamento 3D de alto nível.

---

## 1. Prompt Principal (Pronto para Uso)

**Prompt:**

Create an ultra-premium, highly polished, futuristic **3D corporate technology logo** for the brand **“T-SIM”**, with a cinematic and high-end visual identity.

The composition must feature a **large dominant stylized “T”** on the left and the word **“SIM”** on the right, with the slogan **“DECISÃO INTELIGENTE”** below.

### MAIN SYMBOL — LETTER “T”
Design the **“T”** as a **custom iconic symbol**, not as a regular font character. It must look like an exclusive brand mark, futuristic and premium.

The “T” should have:
- a **broad aerodynamic horizontal top bar**;
- a **twisted ribbon-like vertical body**;
- elegant flowing transitions between surfaces;
- a sense of motion, energy, intelligence, and precision engineering.

The structure must combine:
- **brushed silver metal** on major surfaces;
- **electric blue and cyan luminous technology elements**;
- a **central inner energy flow** running diagonally through the “T”;
- **micro digital/circuit details** embedded inside the blue sections.

The “T” should look like a fusion of:
- brushed aluminium,
- chrome precision parts,
- illuminated data channels,
- advanced software/AI/simulation branding.

The top-left section of the “T” must be **light brushed silver metallic**.
The right side of the top bar must transition into **vivid electric blue / cyan glowing metallic material**.
The internal diagonal ribbon should appear to channel **light, energy, and fast-moving digital signals**.

The lower vertical section of the “T” must show:
- glowing blue depth;
- subtle internal circuit traces;
- strong dimensional lighting;
- a polished, premium 3D finish.

Add refined bevels and ultra-clean edges.
The “T” must be the most memorable and dominant element of the logo.

### TYPOGRAPHY — “SIM”
Place the word **“SIM”** to the right of the “T”.

Requirements:
- uppercase letters;
- bold futuristic sans-serif style;
- geometric, clean, powerful, elegant;
- corporate and technological.

The letters should have:
- **brushed silver metallic fill**;
- subtle internal reflections;
- crisp dimensional shading;
- a **thin electric blue outline/glow** integrated around the edges;
- a **very subtle warm orange accent** on selected lower or side edges to add depth and premium contrast.

The outline must remain elegant and refined — not cartoonish, not exaggerated, not thick.

### SLOGAN
Below “SIM”, write exactly:

**DECISÃO INTELIGENTE**

The slogan must be:
- in uppercase;
- modern sans-serif;
- metallic silver/light grey;
- highly legible;
- evenly spaced;
- aligned with the “SIM” wordmark.

Add a **thin glowing underline** beneath the slogan, with a subtle warm orange-gold highlight in the center, blending softly into the scene.

### BACKGROUND AND SCENE
Use a **dark navy-to-black premium background** with a clean cinematic technology mood.

The background should be:
- elegant;
- dark;
- softly illuminated;
- minimal;
- corporate.

Add a **subtle glossy floor reflection** below the logo to enhance realism and luxury.
Use controlled lens flares and soft bloom around the brightest blue highlights.

### STYLE AND MOOD
The visual style must communicate:
- intelligence;
- simulation;
- AI;
- enterprise software;
- premium SaaS branding;
- innovation;
- digital transformation;
- high-end futuristic identity.

This must look like a **top-tier international software brand logo reveal**, highly polished and visually striking.

### MATERIALS AND FINISH
Use:
- brushed aluminium texture;
- polished chrome-like reflections;
- electric blue light channels;
- cyan light accents;
- ultra-clean anti-aliased edges;
- crisp 3D render quality;
- premium shader realism.

### QUALITY
Render in:
- ultra high definition;
- 4K / 8K quality;
- razor-sharp details;
- perfect typography;
- balanced composition;
- professional brand presentation.

## 3. Estrutura Visual Passo a Passo

### Passo 1 — Construção do “T”
- Criar um símbolo exclusivo, grande e dominante.
- Modelar a parte superior como uma asa/barra aerodinâmica.
- Integrar prata escovado na esquerda e azul brilhante na direita.
- Inserir um fluxo interno em ribbon, com sensação de energia.

### Passo 2 — Miolo tecnológico do “T”
- Adicionar linhas digitais, brilhos azuis, partículas de energia e microcircuitos discretos.
- Fazer com que o interior pareça um canal de dados ou núcleo energético.

### Passo 3 — Tipografia “SIM”
- Usar letras largas, fortes e elegantes.
- Aplicar textura metálica prateada.
- Adicionar contorno azul fino, com pequenos toques quentes sutis nas bordas.

### Passo 4 — Slogan
- Posicionar “DECISÃO INTELIGENTE” abaixo do SIM.
- Usar tipografia limpa, corporativa e espaçada.
- Colocar uma linha horizontal fina luminosa abaixo.

### Passo 5 — Fundo e acabamento
- Fundo escuro premium.
- Reflexo sutil no piso.
- Brilhos controlados nas bordas azuis.
- Manter visual sofisticado e sem poluição.

## 4. Negative Prompt

**Negative Prompt:**

Avoid: cheap logo style, flat design, cartoon look, childish typography, generic letter T, thick orange outline, messy glow, low resolution, distorted text, wrong spelling, noisy background, poor lighting, unbalanced composition, dull materials, plastic appearance, overdone cyberpunk effects, cluttered circuit patterns, amateur branding, fake 2D look, rough edges, blurred details, ugly reflections, excessive lens flare, dark unreadable text, watermark, frame, random symbols, icon clutter.
